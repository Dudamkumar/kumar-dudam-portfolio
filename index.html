01 / ABOUT ME

A lasting journey with
C and C++.

My hands-on experience with C and C++ has shaped how I approach programming: I enjoy understanding how things work, thinking through problems, and staying with a challenge until I have made progress. Winning national-level C/C++ competitions strengthened my confidence, but my interest goes beyond contests. I want to use what I learn to help create practical solutions to real-world problems that affect society.

My journey with C and C++ never ends. I want to keep learning every day, exploring the small details of C and strengthening my understanding one concept at a time. As I learn, I also hope to share my knowledge and help others with coding, while staying curious, adaptable, and persistent when a problem is difficult.

I enjoy treating coding like a game: I imagine scenarios inspired by movies, issues in society, and everyday life, then try to turn them into small programs or problem-solving exercises. I like developing my own approach, writing the code myself, and understanding each step instead of simply copying a solution. Exploring these ideas is enjoyable and keeps my curiosity and motivation strong; it also helps me practise the skills I hope to use in a future C/C++ developer role.

I am a BCA graduate of Sangameshwar College, Solapur. Starting with the fundamentals and reaching my current stage has taught me that progress comes one step at a time. I hope my journey with C and C++ can encourage freshers and fellow developers, and I want to support people who ask me for coding help: listen carefully, understand the problem they are facing, and work through possible solutions with them. I believe confidence should go hand in hand with humility, and I never want to dismiss someone's challenge or act as if I already know everything. My long-term goal is to grow as a C/C++ developer and contribute to meaningful software. I am especially interested in C and C++ opportunities at technology companies such as Microsoft.
